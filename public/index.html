No accounts yet

An account is one OneDrive Personal, one OneDrive Business or one SharePoint document library. Each gets its own folder below the data volume and syncs independently. Three steps:

  1. Add the account. A display name and the type are enough.
  2. Sign in to Microsoft. The station shows a Microsoft link. You sign in on Microsoft's own pages and paste one URL back, your password never touches this UI.
  3. Watch it sync. Syncing starts on its own. The log streams live, and the Folders editor limits what is synced if you do not want everything.
